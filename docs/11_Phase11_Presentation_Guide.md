## 2. Recommended Presentation Styling Guidelines

To ensure your slide deck looks like a professional cybersecurity project:
1. **Color Palette:**
   - Background: Dark Theme (`#0F172A` deep navy/slate) or Clean White (`#FFFFFF`).
   - Primary Accent: Cyber Blue (`#0284C7`) or Wazuh Blue (`#00A4E4`).
   - Severity Accents: Critical Red (`#EF4444`), Warning Orange (`#F97316`), Success Green (`#10B981`).
2. **Typography:**
   - Headings: Bold, modern sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Montserrat*, or *Segoe UI*).
   - Body: Clean, readable (e.g., *Roboto* or *Calibri*, 16–20pt).
3. **Slide Delivery Tips:**
   - Keep slides visual: Place the screenshots from Phase 8 on the right side and bullet points on the left side.
   - Do not read bullet points word-for-word; use the **Speaker Notes** provided for each slide.
   - Limit presentation time to **10–12 minutes**, leaving 3–5 minutes for Q&A.
